First things first

What is Hermes?

Hermes is the open-source AI agent from Nous Research. Like Grok Bot it remembers things, runs routines and uses skills. The big difference: it runs on your own computer, and you choose which AI model it uses.

FreeOpen source, the app costs nothing
Your computerYour data stays with you
Any modelGrok, ChatGPT, DeepSeek…
No region lockWorks where Muse doesn't

On November 2, 2026, Hermes 0.22.6 added an official Grok Bot importer, but it runs in a terminal. Homer Mover turns the whole thing into one window: a few clicks to install, sign in and move.

You end up with: an assistant that replies in your language, plus 5 skills: Grok Bot move, getting-started wizard, Homer Doctor, token saving and images. Just settings and skills, source on GitHub, nothing sent anywhere.

Step 1

Open the app

Unzip the download. The first time, right-click “Homer Mover” → Open → Open.

macOS says “unidentified developer” because the app isn't signed with an Apple developer certificate yet. The source is public on GitHub.

Step 2

Install Hermes (if you don't have it)

Click “Install Hermes”. The app downloads Nous Research's official installer; click through it, about 5–10 minutes. When you see “HERMES IS READY”, don't click Launch Hermes yet. Close it and go back; the app continues by itself and adds Homer's setup.

Step 3

Pick your AI: use an account you already have

Click the one you have: Grok (SuperGrok / X Premium+), ChatGPT (Plus / Pro), OpenRouter, or Nous Portal for a free account. “More options” adds MiniMax (free, tight limits) and “I have an API key” (DeepSeek, Gemini, OpenAI, Claude and more). Your browser opens and the app shows a sign-in code; just approve. No keys to copy. The app marks one “Suggested” based on what you already use.

Step 4

Move your Grok Bot (work files included)

Click Start → Copy → Open Grok Bot, pick the bot, paste and send. The bot makes two files; download both and the app finds them.

The app lists what will move. Click Move: setup, skills, memories, routines, work files and a handoff, all in one go, and it tells you what's still open. Click “Move another bot” for the next one.

Last

Open Hermes and start

Click “Open Hermes”; a “Homer” bot appears under Bots. Type “hi” in the chat box. The getting-started wizard asks three questions (what you do, what it should do for you every day, where you want results) and sets up routines, running each once so you can see it.

It also offers Homer Doctor: every Sunday it checks your setup, routines, memory and token use, explains problems in plain words, and always asks before fixing anything.

Good to know:
  • Routines arrive paused so nothing runs twice. Turn yours off in Grok Bot, then tell the assistant “turn on the XX routine”.
  • Conversations come over as the bot's own summary, not word for word. The originals stay in Grok Bot.
  • The bot has to reply to the pasted message, so this uses a little Grok Bot usage. If you're fully out, wait for the reset.
  • Website and app logins don't move; sign in again in Hermes.

03 Save tokens

5 ways to save tokens

  1. New topic, new chat: every message re-reads the whole conversation, so long chats get expensive.
  2. Don't switch models mid-chat: the next message re-reads everything at full price.
  3. Compress earlier: Homer compresses at 40% of the context (Hermes default is 50%).
  4. Cheap settings for chores: titles and compression run at low reasoning effort.
  5. Cheaper model for everyday chat: tell the assistant “help me save tokens”; it checks your last 7 days first.
Careful: sites selling “discount Claude / GPT tokens” are often shared accounts, stolen keys or swapped-in cheap models, and your chats pass through their servers. Don't use them.

FAQ

FAQ

Mac says “can't be opened” or “unidentified developer”?

Right-click “Homer Mover” → Open → Open; it won't ask again. The app isn't signed with an Apple developer certificate (US$99/year) yet; it isn't malware. The source is on GitHub.

Windows? Intel Mac?

Windows is coming. Intel Macs can't run it because official Hermes Desktop only supports Apple silicon (M1 or later).

No Grok subscription?

Fine. In step 3 pick ChatGPT (Plus / Pro) or OpenRouter, or Nous Portal for a free account (choose the Free plan, then pick a model marked free).

HTTP 403 after signing in to Grok?

A limit on some xAI plans, not your mistake. Use an xAI API key (from console.x.ai) under “More options”.

Do routines run when my computer is off?

No. Hermes Desktop runs on your computer, so it has to be on. Keep always-on jobs in Grok Bot.

Why no Claude or Qwen sign-in?

Claude: Anthropic's terms don't allow Claude subscription logins in other apps; you could be charged extra or suspended. Qwen: its free sign-in ended in April 2026. If you have a Claude or other API key, paste it under “More options”.

Want every message word for word?

The community tool gbot migrate (by sam_lung2077, akari-sora/grok-bot-cli) moves the full transcript and files without using Grok Bot usage. It needs a terminal and Node.js, is unofficial, and reads the Grok Bot app's login data.

Does my data go to Homer?

No. Homer is only settings and skills, open source. Your chats only travel between your computer and the AI you choose; API keys stay on your computer.

How do I update?

Open the newer app; it updates the skills without touching your chats, memory or AI settings.
